ASHI — 75-second walkthrough
All examples and results are fictional. No clinical benefit is claimed.

0–15 seconds · Try practice
A friend asks, “Is there room for one more?” Try a response and reveal a clue. ASHI practices possibilities; a clue cannot tell us everything another person thinks or feels.

15–30 seconds · Capture a moment
An adult selects what happened, where, and the other person's role. The saved summary uses roles rather than real names. This demo keeps everything in memory.

30–45 seconds · Review practice
The child’s account owner reviews moment-based practice. Try approving, editing or rejecting the sample. This is fixed example content, not a live AI call or professional approval.

45–60 seconds · Check transfer
App scores and everyday observations answer different questions. Record whether there was an opportunity to use the skill. No opportunity gets no success or failure rating.

60–75 seconds · Make it fit
School grade, reading grade, plain wording and adaptive support are separate. Try the learning-profile editor. Demo changes do not affect any real account or earned skill evidence.
